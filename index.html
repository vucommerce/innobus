About the Conference

The evolving landscape of India's fiscal architecture demands renewed scholarly attention, especially at a time when the nation stands at the threshold of a structural economic transformation. The introduction of Goods and Services Tax (GST) in 2017 marked a watershed moment in indirect taxation, bringing significant changes to tax administration, compliance, revenue generation, and inter-governmental fiscal relations.

Simultaneously, India’s integration with global financial markets is deepening. Capitals flows - FDI, FPI, external commercial borrowings, remittances, and fintech-driven cross-border transactions - play a pivotal role in influencing domestic investment, macroeconomic stability, exchange rate dynamics, and overall economic growth. India’s moves toward GST 2.0 and capital flows collectively shape the trajectory of the country’s development, especially in the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.

Against this backdrop, the proposed seminar “INNOBUS 2027- International Conference on Innovative Business Practices” seeks to bring together academicians, policymakers, tax experts, industry practitioners, and research scholars to critically evaluate the future of India’s taxation ecosystem and its interaction with capital flow patterns.

About the Department

Vidyasagar University is a reputed university in the Eastern region of India which has been catering to the needs of higher education since 1985. The Department of Commerce, among the oldest departments in the varsity presently offers M.Com. and Ph.D. degrees. The department presently has a team of seven full-time Professors, each of whom has a Doctoral degree.

The department publishes a journal, ‘Vidyasagar University Journal of Commerce’ (ISSN 0973-5917) which is indexed with Citefactor.org and EBSCO. The department also takes the credit of organizing Special Lectures regularly and conducting Research Methodology Workshops. This year the department is organising the 28th Annual Conference.
