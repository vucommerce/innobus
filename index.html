About the Conference

International Conference on Innovative Business Practices

The rapidly evolving global business environment, driven by technological advancements, digital transformation, changing consumer preferences, increasing globalisation and sustainability concerns, has created new opportunities as well as challenges for businesses and institutions. Traditional approaches to business management, finance, marketing, accounting and entrepreneurship are increasingly being reshaped by innovation, emerging technologies and changing socio-economic priorities. In this context, the adoption of innovative, sustainable, inclusive and ethically responsible business practices has become essential for achieving long-term organisational success and sustainable economic development.

The International Conference on Innovative Business Practices aims to provide an interdisciplinary academic and professional platform for researchers, academicians, industry experts, policymakers, entrepreneurs, research scholars and students from India and abroad to deliberate on emerging trends, innovative strategies and contemporary developments in the field of business and management.

The conference seeks to encourage the exchange of knowledge, research findings, practical experiences and innovative ideas concerning the application of emerging technologies, financial innovations, sustainable business models, entrepreneurship, digital marketing, human resource transformation, corporate governance, international trade, business analytics and inclusive economic growth. Special emphasis will be placed on understanding how businesses can leverage technological advancements, promote environmental and social responsibility, enhance operational efficiency and respond effectively to emerging global challenges.

About the Organiser

The INNOBUS 2027 is being organised by Department of Commerce, Vidyasagar University, Midnapore, West Bengal.

Motto: Established in 1985, the Department of Commerce, Vidyasagar University, has a distinguished tradition of postgraduate teaching, research and academic engagement. Guided by its motto, “One Student One Appropriate Employment,” the Department strives to develop competent professionals, responsible entrepreneurs and socially conscious citizens through quality education, research and skill-oriented learning.

Teaching-Learning: The Department offers postgraduate and doctoral research opportunities in Commerce, with academic interests encompassing Accounting, Finance, Banking, Insurance, Taxation, Capital Markets, Corporate Governance and emerging areas of business and management. It emphasises the integration of theoretical knowledge with practical understanding, analytical skills and professional competencies.

Research Activities: Research is an integral part of the Department's academic activities. Faculty members have undertaken UGC- and ICSSR-sponsored research projects and contributed extensively to national and international scholarly literature. Their academic contributions include research articles in reputed journals, authored and edited books, book chapters and participation in editorial activities. The Vidyasagar University Journal of Commerce is an important part of its scholarly engagement.

Organisation of Academic Programmes: The Department regularly organises national and international conferences, seminars, research methodology workshops and extension lectures involving eminent academicians, researchers and industry professionals. These initiatives provide opportunities for intellectual exchange, research dissemination and exposure to contemporary developments in business and the economy.

Career Development: The Department attaches considerable importance to students' career development through placement initiatives, professional interactions and skill-enhancement activities. Its graduates pursue careers in academia, banking, financial services, corporate organisations, government and entrepreneurship.

Beyond Academic Activities: the Department encourages NSS participation, community engagement, village adoption programmes and other socially relevant initiatives. It also promotes alumni interaction and institutional collaboration.

Aspiration: the Department aims to strengthen industry–academia partnerships, interdisciplinary research, international collaborations, digital and financial literacy, entrepreneurship and sustainable development. Through its commitment to academic excellence, research innovation and societal engagement, the Department aspires to contribute meaningfully to inclusive economic growth and nation-building.
